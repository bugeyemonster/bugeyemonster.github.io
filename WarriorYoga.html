Warrior Dance Vinyasa Flow

Target Session Time: 90 Minutes

Welcome to your Warrior Yoga Sequence. This web app will guide you through all 30 poses of the sequence.

Standard poses are timed for 60 seconds (approx. 5 slow breaths). The system automatically inserts a 45-second Vinyasa Transition between major standing movements to allow you to deliberately move through Chaturanga, Upward Dog, and Downward Dog.

Use the controls to Pause or Skip as needed. This practice is not a substitute for medical advice; listen to your body and skip or modify any pose that does not feel safe.
